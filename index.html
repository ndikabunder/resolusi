Glyphguardian

Echoes of Light

As the last guardian, use your sacred bow and ancient Glyphs to defend the Core of Light from endless waves of shadow creatures.

How to Play

Controls:

WASD - Move Glyphguardian

Mouse - Aim

Left Click - Shoot Arrow

1-4 - Place Glyphs

Space - Special Ability

Gameplay:

• Defend the Aethel Core from shadow creatures

• Place Glyphs strategically to control enemies

• Earn Lumen to upgrade between waves

• Survive as long as possible!
